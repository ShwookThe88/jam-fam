# Jam Fam — future ideas

User-requested ideas for later; these are not implemented features.

## Main product goal: find online bandmates

Jam together and find your band. Help players meet compatible musicians through playing and building songs together.

- Let players discover collaborators through shared Songbook contributions and invitations to add a part.
- Future player profiles can include instruments, genres, skill level, and whether they are looking for a band.
- Invite musicians whose playing fits to another session.
- Form a regular crew and continue building songs together.

Bandmate discovery, profiles, invitations, and persistent crews are planned, not implemented.

## Other future ideas

- A progression from beginner to expert cover-song challenges.
- Beginner example: “Blitzkrieg Bop” by the Ramones.
- Expert example: “Linoleum” by NOFX.
- Bands can “book” virtual shows once they have practiced enough.
- Shows could use a chosen setlist and the band’s multiplayer instruments.

Implemented: eight-genre free jam; three drum lessons and a solo soundcheck with local skill progress; a two-player-or-more drum show with a shared crowd score; a device-local microphone Riff Bank; and a persistent Songbook for shared instrument loops.

Still future work: cover-song/tab lessons, instrument paths beyond drums, virtual show booking, microphone layers in shared songs, and assigning Riff Bank recordings to custom sample pads.

## Beat the Streets — later community challenge

- Players submit a 60-second beat preview, with an optional complete version.
- Community voting picks the winning beat.
- Potential non-cash reward: an Encore Pass or feature unlock.
- With the beat creator's permission, the winning beat becomes a later Freestyle Battle backing.
- Freestyle players submit performances over that shared beat for another community vote.
- Earned points and passes reward challenge progress; they are not by themselves a source of funding.

These community submission, voting, and reward features are planned, not implemented.
- A future winner spotlight inside Jam Fam could feature the artist and link to their SoundCloud. This is a planned in-app promotion, not promised paid advertising on SoundCloud.

## Real guitar input — first implementation

- Real guitar source is available for both guitar slots in Free jam.
- USB audio device selection, mono channel selection or stereo inputs 1+2 for pedal effects, input meter, and recording gain.
- BOSS GT-1 USB setup guidance; pedal/interface monitoring recommended, optional browser monitoring off by default.
- Guitar-only takes up to three minutes save to the existing device-local Riff Bank and can be played back or downloaded.
- Not yet implemented: live audio transmission to bandmates, recorded audio layers in shared Songbook, amp simulation, or automatic latency alignment.
- Hardware validation with the user's GT-1 remains necessary.

## Submission audio cleanup

Jazz Groove, Rock Groove, and Piano Blues 1 (Corel recordings) were removed from the app's track choices and hosted audio assets at the user's request. Existing rooms and Songbook entries referencing them use an original synthesized groove in the same genre; saved instrument parts are preserved. The uploaded source files are untouched. No supplied MIDI song files are bundled with the app.

Backing cleanup: automatic synth-guitar chord stabs and repeated rim/crash/clap accents are removed from generated backing tracks. Core kick/snare/hi-hat and bass remain. User-played instruments, saved parts, and practice/count-in cues are unchanged. User listening confirmation of the reported clunk is still needed.
